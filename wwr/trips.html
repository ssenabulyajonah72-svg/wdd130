MU2K WHITEWATER ADVENTURES

The Nile High Adventure


Location: Jinja, Uganda (The Source of the Nile)

Description: Take on legendary Grade 5 rapids on the mighty White Nile. Plunge through massive warm-water drops like "The Bad Place" and "Overtime," led by expert guides who ensure an adrenaline-packed yet safe ride through Africa's most iconic whitewater.

The Grand Canyon Expedition


Location: Colorado River, Arizona, USA

Description: Experience a multi-day wilderness journey through towering red rock canyons. Navigate famous rapids like Lava Falls and Crystal, camp under breathtaking desert stars, and explore hidden side canyons on one of the world's greatest river runs.

The Wild Rivers Run


Location: Shotover River, Queenstown, New Zealand

Description: Blast through dramatic, narrow rock canyons surrounded by breathtaking alpine scenery. This fast-paced adventure combines intense rapids with stunning New Zealand landscapes, offering an unforgettable thrill for both beginners and seasoned thrill-seekers.
